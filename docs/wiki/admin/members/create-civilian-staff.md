# Create Civilian Staff

> Audience: Admins

![Create civilian staff form](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/members-create-civilian.png)

## What This Is

Create Civilian Staff adds authorized non-member personnel who need Sentinel handling without being treated as nominal-roll members.

## When To Use It

- Museum staff or approved civilian staff need badge/check-in support.
- The person is not a nominal-roll member.
- A civilian staff record is needed for operational attendance.

## What To Check

- Authorization source for the civilian staff record.
- Name, organization or division context, and access need.
- Whether badge access is allowed.

## Step-By-Step Procedure

1. Confirm the person should be civilian staff, not a member.
2. Open New Civilian Staff.
3. Enter identity and contact details.
4. Choose the correct status and access fields.
5. Save, then verify the record appears under the expected scope.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: The record supports operational use without misclassifying the person.

Caution: Civilian staff can appear in operational views, so naming and access must be clear.

Stop: Do not use civilian staff records to bypass member-account requirements.

## Confirm After Action

- The row scope and status are correct.
- Badge state matches authorization.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if the person should instead be handled as a visitor or full member.
