# DDS: Transfer DDS

> Audience: outgoing DDS, incoming DDS, duty supervisors, Admin users

![Transfer DDS action](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-action-transfer-dds.png)

Use Transfer DDS when live DDS responsibility must move from one person to another.

## What This Is

DDS transfer changes who Sentinel treats as holding Duty Day Staff responsibility. It is an operational responsibility change, not a cosmetic update.

## When To Use It

- Weekly DDS handover.
- Outgoing DDS is leaving.
- Incoming DDS is taking over.
- Scheduled DDS is unavailable and a replacement is assigned.
- Admin needs to correct live DDS after a verified mismatch.

## Before You Transfer

Confirm:

- Current live DDS.
- Intended receiving DDS.
- Receiving member is checked in and DDS-qualified.
- Building Status and lockup holder are understood.
- Both people agree to the handover, unless an Admin override is required.

## Step-By-Step DDS Procedure

1. Open Dashboard.
2. Read the DDS status block.
3. Select the DDS action, such as Transfer DDS or Complete Handover.
4. Choose the receiving DDS.
5. Read the confirmation details carefully.
6. Confirm the transfer.
7. Recheck the DDS status block.
8. Confirm the new DDS understands lockup and building state.

## Good / Caution / Stop

Good: The receiving DDS is present, qualified, checked in, and expecting responsibility.

Caution: Weekly handover may show outgoing DDS as live and incoming DDS as scheduled. That is normal until the transfer is recorded.

Stop: The receiving DDS is absent, not checked in, not qualified, or does not agree to accept responsibility.
