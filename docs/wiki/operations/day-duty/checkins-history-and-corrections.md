# Check-ins History and Corrections

> Audience: Duty Day Staff (DDS), managers, admins

![Check-ins history table with correction controls](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/checkins-corrections-main.png)

## What This Is

Check-ins History is the audit view for member and visitor attendance events. Use it to review what Sentinel recorded and to apply controlled corrections only when the real operational record requires it.

Corrections change historical records, so they must be treated as auditable operational actions.

## When To Use It

- A badge scan was missed or duplicated.
- A visitor or member was recorded in the wrong direction.
- DDS needs to prove when someone entered or left.
- A supervisor asks for attendance evidence.
- Dashboard Presence does not match the real building state after search and refresh.

## What To Check

- Sentinel says which event happened, when it happened, and how it was recorded.
- The real person/building state confirms whether the record is correct.
- The date, direction, person, and kiosk/source are the exact record needing review.
- You have evidence before editing: direct confirmation, duty desk log, visitor sheet, camera/access record, or supervisor instruction.

## Step-By-Step DDS Procedure

1. Filter to the smallest useful scope: date, direction, division, member, or visitor type.
2. Find the exact record. Do not edit a nearby record because the name looks similar.
3. Compare Sentinel's record to the real situation and available evidence.
4. If a correction is allowed, open the edit action for that row.
5. Change only the fields needed to make the record accurate.
6. Save the correction and confirm the row now shows the expected value.
7. Recheck Dashboard Presence if the correction affects who is on site.
8. Leave a note or escalate if the record cannot be corrected confidently.

## Good / Caution / Stop

Good: The correction is narrow, evidence-backed, and the resulting history matches what actually happened.

Caution: The person has multiple entries close together, a visitor group has several members, or the correction changes lockup readiness. Ask a supervisor before saving.

Stop: Do not correct a record when the evidence is missing, the person identity is uncertain, the system is unhealthy, or the change would hide an unresolved visitor/member presence issue.

## Confirm After Action

- The corrected row displays the expected timestamp, direction, person, method, and status.
- Dashboard Presence now matches the real building state.
- The correction did not change adjacent records.
- Any high-impact correction is communicated to watch leadership.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if a record is missing entirely, multiple records conflict, visitor checkout is unclear, the correction affects lockup, or Sentinel says someone is on site but DDS cannot confirm where they are.
