# Visitor Sign-In

> Audience: Duty Day Staff (DDS), watch supervisors, kiosk operators

![Visitor sign-in review page with vehicle information](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/visitor-sign-in.png)

## What This Is

Visitor Sign-In records who a visitor is, why they are at the unit, whether they are connected to a member or event, and whether any vehicle is associated with the visit.

Use this page when a visitor is checking in through the kiosk or when DDS is supervising the visitor flow.

## When To Use It

- A visitor arrives for recruitment, a meeting, an event, museum business, contract work, delivery, or another approved reason.
- DDS needs to confirm the visitor record appears correctly in Sentinel.
- A visitor has vehicle information that should be recorded before they enter.
- The visitor flow needs staff support because the visitor is unsure what to select.

## What To Check

- Sentinel says the kiosk is connected and ready.
- The visitor is physically present and has an approved reason to be at the unit.
- The selected visit reason matches the real visit.
- Names, organization or unit, host/member/event, and vehicle plate are correct before finishing.
- DDS knows who is responsible for checkout before the visitor leaves.

## Step-By-Step DDS Procedure

1. Start the visitor sign-in flow from the kiosk Visitor Services panel.
2. Ask the visitor why they are at the unit and choose the matching reason.
3. Select the member or event if the flow asks for one.
4. Enter the visitor name and required details.
5. Add every vehicle connected to the visitor group before review.
6. Review the summary with the visitor.
7. Finish sign-in only after the visitor confirms the details are correct.
8. Open Dashboard Presence and confirm the visitor appears under Visitors.

## Good / Caution / Stop

Good: The visitor reason is clear, the review page matches what the visitor says, and the visitor appears in Dashboard Presence after sign-in.

Caution: The visitor is unsure of the host, event, organization, vehicle plate, or purpose. Pause and ask DDS or the watch supervisor before finishing.

Stop: Do not finish sign-in if the visitor is not authorized, refuses required information, appears under the wrong reason, or should not enter until someone approves the visit.

## Confirm After Action

- Dashboard Presence shows the visitor count increased.
- The visitor card has the correct name and organization or reason.
- Vehicle information appears in the review/history context when entered.
- DDS knows whether the visitor must be checked out from the kiosk or from Dashboard.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if Sentinel says the visitor is checked in but the person is not present, the visitor is still listed at lockup, the visitor reason is unclear, or the visitor cannot be matched to the expected member/event.
