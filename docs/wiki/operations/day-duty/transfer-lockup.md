# DDS: Transfer Lockup

> Audience: DDS, lockup holder, duty supervisors

![Transfer Lockup action](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-action-transfer-lockup.png)

Use Transfer Lockup when responsibility for securing the building moves to another qualified checked-in member.

## What This Is

Lockup transfer changes who owns responsibility for securing the building. The person receiving lockup must understand the building state and accept responsibility.

## When To Use It

- Current lockup holder is leaving before lockup.
- DDS is handing lockup to another qualified member.
- Responsibility moves during a handoff.
- The wrong person is shown as holder and the correct holder is verified.

## Before You Transfer

Confirm:

- Current lockup holder.
- Receiving member is checked in.
- Receiving member is qualified to hold lockup.
- Receiving member agrees to accept lockup.
- Building Status is understood.
- No security alert blocks the transfer.

## Step-By-Step DDS Procedure

1. Open Dashboard.
2. Confirm the Lockup Holder status block.
3. Open the Transfer Lockup action.
4. Select the receiving member.
5. Enter the reason if prompted.
6. Confirm transfer.
7. Recheck Lockup Holder.
8. Tell the receiving member that Sentinel now shows them as holder.

## Good / Caution / Stop

Good: Holder and receiver both agree, receiver is checked in and qualified, and Sentinel updates the holder block.

Caution: No qualified checked-in receiver is available. Do not force a transfer to someone who cannot hold lockup.

Stop: Current holder identity is disputed, receiver is absent, or building state is unclear.
