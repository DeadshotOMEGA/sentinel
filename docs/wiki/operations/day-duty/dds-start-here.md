# DDS Start Here

> Audience: Duty Day Staff (DDS), incoming DDS, outgoing DDS, duty supervisors

[Open Sentinel Dashboard](http://sentinel.local/dashboard)

![Dashboard overview for DDS operations](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-overview-main.png)

Use this page when you are the DDS and need the shortest safe path through Sentinel. It starts at login and ends with handoff, lockup transfer, or executed lockup.

## What DDS Uses Sentinel For

DDS uses Sentinel to answer four operational questions:

1. Is Sentinel healthy enough to trust?
2. Who does Sentinel say is responsible for DDS, Duty Watch, the building, and lockup?
3. Who does Sentinel say is in the building?
4. What action is safe now: continue, correct, transfer, lock up, or escalate?

## DDS Work Order

1. Confirm you are signed into the correct Sentinel system.
2. Check System Status before trusting counts or pressing actions.
3. Review Security Alerts before changing building or lockup state.
4. Confirm DDS and Duty Watch coverage.
5. Confirm Building Status and Lockup Holder match the real building.
6. Review Presence, including visitors.
7. Use the required action only after Sentinel and reality agree.
8. Confirm the result in the Dashboard.
9. Escalate mismatches instead of forcing the Dashboard to look correct.

## Stop Conditions

Stop and escalate if:

- System Status is unhealthy and the data looks stale.
- Security Alerts affect building security, visitors, or remaining people.
- Sentinel says the building is open but the real building is secured, or the reverse.
- Lockup holder identity is disputed or the holder cannot be contacted.
- A visitor is still checked in near lockup and cannot be confirmed.
- DDS or Duty Watch coverage is unclear.
