# DDS: Presence, Visitors, And Corrections

> Audience: DDS, watch supervisors, authorized Admin users

![Presence grid for DDS review](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-presence-grid-focus.png)

Use this whenever you need to prove who Sentinel believes is on site.

## What This Is

Presence is the operational count of checked-in members and visitors. It is not a guess. DDS should use it to confirm people before handoff, visitor checkout, manual correction, transfer, and lockup.

## Common DDS Presence Tasks

| Task                     | How to do it                                                               |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Find a member            | Use Search by rank, last name, or first name.                              |
| Find a visitor           | Use the Visitors filter, then search by name or organization.              |
| Confirm duty staff       | Search by name or review role badges on cards.                             |
| Correct a missed scan    | Verify the missed event, use Manual In/Out, and record the reason.         |
| Check a visitor out      | Open the visitor action or checkout affordance after confirming departure. |
| Confirm lockup readiness | Filter Visitors first, then All, then Members.                             |

## Manual Correction Rules

Only correct attendance when you know what really happened.

Acceptable evidence may include:

- The person is physically present in front of you.
- A supervisor confirms the person departed.
- A scanner failure happened and was observed.
- A visitor checkout was missed but departure was verified.

Do not manually correct when:

- You are guessing.
- The person cannot be contacted.
- A visitor may still be in the building.
- The correction affects lockup or security and the responsible person disagrees.

## Visitor Handling

Visitors need extra attention because they may not know Sentinel procedures.

1. Confirm the visitor's purpose and host.
2. Ensure visitor sign-in is complete.
3. Confirm they appear in Presence.
4. Before lockup, search Visitors first.
5. Check out visitors only after confirming they have departed.

## Good / Caution / Stop

Good: Presence matches the people in the building, visitor status is clear, and corrections have a verified reason.

Caution: Filters may hide people. Clear filters and search before deciding no one is present.

Stop: A visitor remains checked in near lockup and cannot be found or confirmed departed.
