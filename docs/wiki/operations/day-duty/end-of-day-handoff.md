# DDS: End Of Day Handoff

> Audience: outgoing DDS, incoming DDS, duty supervisors

![Presence review before end-of-day handoff](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-presence-grid-focus.png)

Use this before leaving, handing over, or closing the operational day.

## What This Is

End-of-day handoff confirms that Sentinel and the real building still agree before DDS signs out, transfers responsibility, transfers lockup, or executes lockup.

## DDS Handoff Checklist

1. Review unresolved Security Alerts.
2. Search Presence for visitors.
3. Search Presence for unexpected members.
4. Confirm Duty Watch expectations.
5. Confirm DDS responsibility after you leave.
6. Confirm Lockup Holder.
7. Transfer DDS if responsibility is moving.
8. Transfer Lockup if lockup responsibility is moving.
9. Execute Lockup only if the building should be secured.
10. Leave notes or escalate if anything does not match reality.

## Note Examples

Use direct notes:

- "Visitor Smith departed at 1540; manual checkout recorded after missed kiosk checkout."
- "S1 Jones remains checked in for authorized tasking; duty supervisor aware."
- "Lockup transferred from MS Voth to PO2 Wright at 1620; receiver confirmed."
- "Building Status did not update after lockup attempt; screenshot captured and escalated."

## Good / Caution / Stop

Good: Alerts are handled, Presence is reviewed, DDS and lockup responsibilities are known, and the next person understands the state.

Caution: Some people remain checked in for known reasons. Confirm and document before you leave.

Stop: Unresolved alerts, unaccounted visitors, disputed lockup holder, or unclear DDS handoff.
