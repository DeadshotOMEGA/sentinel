# DDS: Daily Start

> Audience: DDS, incoming DDS, duty supervisors

![Status panel for DDS start checks](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-status-panel-focus.png)

Use this at the beginning of the operational day, before assuming the building, people count, or duty state is correct.

## What This Is

Daily Start is the DDS opening routine inside Sentinel. It verifies system health, alerts, DDS ownership, Duty Watch coverage, building state, lockup responsibility, and Presence.

## DDS Start Checklist

1. Confirm [System Status](/operations/dashboard/daily-start/system-status) is Healthy.
2. Review [Security Alerts](/operations/dashboard/daily-start/security-alerts).
3. Confirm DDS status: scheduled DDS, live DDS, and handover state.
4. Confirm Duty Watch coverage and uncovered positions.
5. Confirm Building Status matches the real building.
6. Confirm Lockup Holder is known.
7. Review Presence for unexpected members, visitors, or missing duty staff.
8. Use search and filters before deciding someone is absent.
9. Escalate unresolved gaps before operations begin.

## Open Building

Use Open Building only when the building is actually open or about to be opened by the responsible person.

Before opening in Sentinel:

- Confirm the real building state.
- Confirm who should hold lockup responsibility after opening.
- Confirm DDS state is understood.
- Confirm no alert blocks opening.

After opening:

- Building Status should show Open.
- Lockup Holder should match the responsible member.
- Presence should reflect the people who are actually on site.

## Good / Caution / Stop

Good: System Status is Healthy, alerts are clear or being handled, DDS is known, Duty Watch coverage is understood, Building Status matches reality, and Presence looks reasonable.

Caution: The scheduled DDS and live DDS differ. This may be normal during weekly handover, but verify before continuing.

Stop: No one knows who holds DDS or lockup, an alert affects security, or Sentinel and the real building disagree.
