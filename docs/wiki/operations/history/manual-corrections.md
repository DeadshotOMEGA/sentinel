# Manual History Corrections

> Audience: DDS, managers, admins

![Manual check-in correction modal](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/history-manual-correction-modal.png)

## What This Is

Manual corrections create or repair records when Sentinel missed a verified movement event.

## When To Use It

- A badge scan was missed.
- A member was checked in or out by an authorized manual process.
- A correction is required after confirming evidence.

## What To Check

- Sentinel says the movement is missing or wrong.
- The real person/building state proves what should have happened.
- You have a reason that can be explained during an audit.

## Step-By-Step Procedure

1. Search History first so you do not duplicate an existing row.
2. Open Manual Check-In/Out.
3. Choose the person, direction, and timestamp.
4. Enter a clear reason based on evidence.
5. Save, then confirm the History table and Dashboard Presence if live occupancy changed.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: The correction is narrow, evidence-backed, and easy to explain later.

Caution: Manual corrections can change whether Sentinel believes someone is on site.

Stop: Do not manually check someone out to make lockup look ready unless the person is actually gone.

## Confirm After Action

- The new or updated row appears in History.
- Dashboard Presence matches the real building state.
- DDS or supervisor notes mention the correction if it affected operations.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if identity, direction, or timestamp cannot be proven.
