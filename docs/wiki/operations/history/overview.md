# Check-In/Out History Overview

> Audience: DDS, managers, admins

![Check-In/Out History page overview](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/history-overview.png)

## What This Is

History is the audit view for member and visitor check-in and check-out records. It is used to prove what Sentinel recorded after the live moment has passed.

## When To Use It

- Review who checked in or out during a date range.
- Investigate a Presence mismatch after checking the Dashboard.
- Prepare evidence for a supervisor, DDS handoff, visitor question, or correction.

## What To Check

- Sentinel says who moved, when, direction, method, kiosk/source, and review status.
- The real person/building state confirms whether the record is correct.
- You are looking at History for evidence, not Presence for live occupancy.

## Step-By-Step Procedure

1. Open History from the navbar or page help.
2. Choose the smallest date and direction range that can answer the question.
3. Review records before editing anything.
4. If a record affects live occupancy, confirm the Dashboard Presence view after the review.
5. Escalate instead of guessing when the record and real situation do not line up.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: The records explain the situation without requiring a correction.

Caution: A filtered view can hide the record you need. Clear filters before deciding a record is missing.

Stop: Do not make a correction when the person identity, timestamp, or evidence is uncertain.

## Confirm After Action

- The History row matches the real evidence.
- Any live occupancy impact is checked again in Dashboard Presence.
- Notes or escalation are captured when reality and Sentinel still disagree.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if records conflict, a visitor checkout is unclear, or correcting the record would affect lockup readiness.
