# History Filters

> Audience: DDS, managers, admins

![History filters for date, direction, and people records](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/history-filters.png)

## What This Is

History filters narrow the audit record list so you can find the exact event before reading or correcting it.

## When To Use It

- A date range has too many records.
- You need only check-ins, check-outs, a division, or a person.
- A supervisor asks for a specific window of evidence.

## What To Check

- The date range includes the possible mistake.
- Direction is not excluding the record you expect.
- Division or member filters are cleared when searching across the whole unit.

## Step-By-Step Procedure

1. Start with date first.
2. Add direction only when you are certain the record should be in or out.
3. Add division or person filters only after the date range is correct.
4. Review the count and table after each filter change.
5. Clear filters before declaring that no record exists.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: The record set is small enough to review confidently.

Caution: Sentinel says no results, but the real situation suggests a record should exist. A filter may be hiding it.

Stop: Do not create a manual correction until you have checked that filters are not hiding the real record.

## Confirm After Action

- The table shows the expected range.
- The filtered result includes the exact person or visitor group you are checking.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if the correct record cannot be found after clearing filters and refreshing the page.
