# Event Duty Watch

> Audience: Event planners, schedulers, admins

![Event Duty Watch assignment area](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/events-duty-watch.png)

## What This Is

Event Duty Watch handles support positions for a specific event, separate from routine weekly Duty Watch scheduling.

## When To Use It

- An event requires support positions.
- A planner needs to assign or review event-specific coverage.
- DDS needs to know who supports an event.

## What To Check

- Event details are correct before assigning people.
- Required positions are known.
- Assigned members are eligible and available.

## Step-By-Step Procedure

1. Open the event detail page.
2. Open the Duty Watch tab.
3. Review required positions.
4. Assign eligible members to each position.
5. Confirm unassigned positions are intentional or escalated.
6. Update assignments if the event changes.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Every required event position has an eligible assigned member or an explicit unresolved note.

Caution: Event Duty Watch does not replace weekly Duty Watch unless your local procedure says it does.

Stop: Do not assign unavailable or unqualified members to make the tab look complete.

## Confirm After Action

- The Duty Watch tab shows expected assignments.
- Schedulers and DDS know about event-specific coverage.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if a required event position cannot be filled.
