# Presence: Member Actions

![Member action panel](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-member-action-panel.png)

Member actions are member-specific controls opened from a person card.

## What This Is

Member actions are member-specific controls opened from a person card.

## When To Use It

Use them when the operation applies to one selected member.

## What To Check

- Selected member identity.
- Available actions.
- Recent activity.
- Any duty, lockup, or role state shown in the panel.

## Step-by-Step Admin Procedure

1. Open the correct member card.
2. Confirm the panel belongs to that member.
3. Choose only the needed action.
4. Complete required notes or confirmations.
5. Recheck the card and status block.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: The panel and action match the intended member.

Caution: The action is available but status should be reviewed first.

Stop: The wrong member panel is open.

## What To Confirm After Action

Confirm the member card and status panel after saving.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if member actions fail or show conflicting state.
