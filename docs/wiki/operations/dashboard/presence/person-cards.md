# Presence: Person Cards

![Representative person card](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-person-card-focus.png)

Person cards are the live attendance records shown in Presence.

## What This Is

Person cards are the live attendance records shown in Presence.

## When To Use It

Use them to confirm identity before member or visitor actions.

## What To Check

- Name, rank, and initials.
- Role badges and tags.
- Duty position or visitor details.
- Last activity time.

## Step-by-Step Admin Procedure

1. Find the person card.
2. Confirm identity details.
3. Check role and duty indicators.
4. Read last activity.
5. Open actions only for the correct card.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Card details clearly match the person.

Caution: Two people have similar names or initials.

Stop: Identity cannot be confirmed.

## What To Confirm After Action

Confirm card state after any action.

## When To Escalate

Escalate identity uncertainty before changing attendance or responsibility.
