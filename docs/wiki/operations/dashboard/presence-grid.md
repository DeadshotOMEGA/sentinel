# Presence Grid

![Presence grid with filter controls and person cards](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-presence-grid-focus.png)

Presence is the live list of people Sentinel believes are currently on site.

## What This Is

Presence helps Admins verify who is in the building. It includes checked-in members and visitors, filters, search, person cards, and member or visitor action controls.

## When To Use It

Use Presence after check-in, checkout, visitor sign-in, visitor sign-out, DDS transfer, lockup transfer, manual correction, and lockup.

## What To Check

- All, Members, and Visitors counts.
- Expected duty staff.
- Unexpected checked-in people.
- Visitors who should be signed out.
- Last activity time on person cards.
- Filters and search before assuming someone is absent.

## Admin Procedure

1. Start with the total count.
2. Filter Members or Visitors if the list is crowded.
3. Search for a specific person before deciding they are absent.
4. Open the correct person card only after confirming identity.
5. Use member or visitor actions only when the real person/state matches the card.
6. Recheck counts after the action.

## Good / Caution / Stop

Good: Presence matches who is physically on site.

Caution: Filters or search may be hiding people.

Stop: Sentinel shows someone present who is not actually present, or someone present in the building does not appear in Sentinel.

## What To Confirm After Action

Confirm the person card, filter count, and relevant status block changed as expected.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if Presence fails to load, if a manual correction is disputed, or if counts remain wrong after search, filter reset, and refresh.
