# Presence Grid

Presence is the live list of people Sentinel believes are on site.

## What to Check

- Members and visitors currently checked in.
- Duty badges such as DDS, active role, or tonight duty.
- Last activity time.
- Visitor host and visit details.

## Admin Action

After check-in, checkout, visitor sign-in, lockup transfer, or manual correction, recheck Presence to confirm the result.
