# Daily Start: Presence Review

![Presence grid reviewed at daily start](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-presence-grid-focus.png)

Presence review confirms who Sentinel believes is on site before the Admin relies on duty or lockup state.

## What This Is

Presence review confirms who Sentinel believes is on site before the Admin relies on duty or lockup state.

## When To Use It

Use it after status checks and before starting routine operations.

## What To Check

- Expected duty staff are visible or explained.
- Unexpected visitors are reviewed.
- Unexpected checked-in members are understood.
- Filters and search have been used before assuming absence.

## Step-by-Step Admin Procedure

1. Read the All, Members, and Visitors counts.
2. Filter to Members to check expected staff.
3. Filter to Visitors to check visitor state.
4. Search for expected people who are not immediately visible.
5. Correct or escalate attendance mismatches.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Presence matches who is physically in the building.

Caution: A person is missing from view, but filters or search may be hiding them.

Stop: A person is physically present but missing from Sentinel, or Sentinel shows someone present who is gone.

## What To Confirm After Action

After correction, confirm the person card and counts update.

## When To Escalate

Escalate unresolved attendance mismatches before relying on duty or lockup state.
