# Daily Start: Escalation

![Dashboard overview used to collect escalation context](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-overview-main.png)

Escalation is the step where an Admin stops guessing and gives leadership or technical support enough context to act.

## What This Is

Escalation is the step where an Admin stops guessing and gives leadership or technical support enough context to act.

## When To Use It

Use it when System Status, alerts, status blocks, Presence, or the real building do not agree.

## What To Check

- What Sentinel says.
- What the real-world situation is.
- Which person, role, badge, or action is affected.
- What was already tried.
- Whether operations can safely continue.

## Step-by-Step Admin Procedure

1. Stop before pressing more actions.
2. Capture a screenshot with the navbar and affected section visible.
3. Write down the time observed.
4. Record the person, role, badge, or system involved.
5. Escalate to watch leadership or technical support according to local procedure.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: The issue is resolved or handed off with enough detail for the next person.

Caution: The issue seems minor, but you cannot explain why Sentinel and reality differ.

Stop: A building, lockup, DDS, alert, or attendance mismatch remains unresolved.

## What To Confirm After Action

Confirm the receiving person understands the current status and next action.

## When To Escalate

Escalate any unresolved state gap before operations begin.
