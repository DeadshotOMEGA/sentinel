# Daily Start: Duty Watch

![Duty Watch readiness block in the status panel](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-stat-duty-watch.png)

Duty Watch readiness shows whether scheduled watch positions are covered by checked-in or live-only people.

## What This Is

Duty Watch readiness shows whether scheduled watch positions are covered by checked-in or live-only people.

## When To Use It

Use it at daily start and before the watch window begins.

## What To Check

- Assigned count matches the expected schedule.
- Checked-in count is enough for the watch.
- Uncovered positions are understood.
- Live-only coverage has been confirmed with the watch team.

## If One Position Is Uncovered

First search Presence for the expected watch member. If they are present but not counted, verify schedule or duty assignment. If they are absent, confirm the replacement before operations depend on that position.

## Step-by-Step Admin Procedure

1. Read the assigned and checked-in counts.
2. Look for uncovered positions.
3. Look for live-only coverage that is not in the schedule.
4. Search Presence for expected watch members.
5. Resolve or escalate gaps before the watch window.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: All required positions are covered by the expected people.

Caution: Coverage exists, but it is live-only or different from the schedule.

Stop: A required position is uncovered and no qualified replacement is confirmed.

## What To Confirm After Action

After a correction, confirm Duty Watch counts and Presence both reflect the coverage.

## When To Escalate

Escalate missing or unclear coverage before operations depend on the watch.
