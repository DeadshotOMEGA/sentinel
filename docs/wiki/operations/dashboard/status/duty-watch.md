# Status: Duty Watch

![Duty Watch status block](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-stat-duty-watch.png)

Duty Watch shows whether scheduled watch positions are covered.

## What This Is

Duty Watch shows whether scheduled watch positions are covered.

## When To Use It

Use it when checking readiness, daily start, evening handoff, or coverage gaps.

## What To Check

- Assigned slots.
- Checked-in duty members.
- Uncovered slots.
- Live-only coverage outside the schedule.

## Step-by-Step Admin Procedure

1. Read assigned and checked-in counts.
2. Identify uncovered slots.
3. Check whether live-only coverage is expected.
4. Search Presence for the duty members.
5. Escalate gaps that cannot be resolved.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Required positions are covered by expected people.

Caution: Coverage exists but does not match the schedule.

Stop: A required watch position is uncovered.

## What To Confirm After Action

Confirm counts update after correction.

## When To Escalate

Escalate missing qualified coverage.
