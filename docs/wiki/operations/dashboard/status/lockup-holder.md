# Status: Lockup Holder

![Lockup Holder block](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-stat-lockup-holder.png)

The lockup holder owns responsibility for securing the building.

## What This Is

The lockup holder owns responsibility for securing the building.

## When To Use It

Use it before transfer, checkout, end-of-day handoff, or Execute Lockup.

## What To Check

- Holder identity.
- Since time.
- Whether transfer is available.
- Whether the real person accepts responsibility.

## Step-by-Step Admin Procedure

1. Read the holder name.
2. Confirm identity with the real person or watch team.
3. Check how long they have held responsibility.
4. Transfer only to a verified qualified person.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Holder identity and responsibility are clear.

Caution: Holder is correct but time held needs explanation.

Stop: Holder is wrong, absent, or disputed.

## What To Confirm After Action

After transfer, confirm the new holder is shown.

## When To Escalate

Escalate disputed lockup responsibility.
