# Status: Quick Action State

![Quick action state in the status panel](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-quick-actions-focus.png)

Quick actions are enabled or disabled based on permissions and current operational state.

## What This Is

Quick actions are enabled or disabled based on permissions and current operational state.

## When To Use It

Use it when a button is missing, disabled, or unexpected.

## What To Check

- Your account permission.
- Building state.
- DDS and lockup holder state.
- Eligible recipients or required people.

## Step-by-Step Admin Procedure

1. Read the disabled or available action.
2. Check the related status block.
3. Fix the missing condition if authorized.
4. Do not look for a workaround.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Available actions match what should be possible.

Caution: An action is disabled because a required condition is missing.

Stop: An action appears available while status or reality disagrees.

## What To Confirm After Action

After fixing a condition, confirm the action state updates.

## When To Escalate

Escalate action-state mismatches or permission confusion.
