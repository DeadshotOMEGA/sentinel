# Daily End: Lockup Holder

![Lockup holder status before lockup](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-stat-lockup-holder.png)

The lockup holder is the person responsible for securing the building.

## What This Is

The lockup holder is the person responsible for securing the building.

## When To Use It

Use it before transfer, checkout, or Execute Lockup.

## What To Check

- The holder name is correct.
- The holder is actually accepting responsibility.
- The time held makes sense.
- Transfer is used if responsibility is moving.

## Step-by-Step Admin Procedure

1. Read the Lockup Holder block.
2. Confirm the holder with the real person or watch team.
3. Check how long they have held lockup.
4. Transfer lockup if someone else is accepting responsibility.
5. Do not execute lockup with an unclear holder.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Sentinel and the real holder match.

Caution: The holder is correct, but the time held looks unusual.

Stop: Sentinel shows the wrong holder or no verified holder exists.

## What To Confirm After Action

After transfer, confirm the Lockup Holder block updates.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if lockup responsibility is disputed or unclear.
