# Daily End: Presence Review

![Presence grid reviewed before lockup](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-presence-grid-focus.png)

End-of-day Presence review prevents lockup while unexpected people remain checked in.

## What This Is

End-of-day Presence review prevents lockup while unexpected people remain checked in.

## When To Use It

Use it after alert review and before confirming lockup holder.

## What To Check

- Visitors are filtered and reviewed.
- Unexpected checked-in members are explained.
- Expected late-stay members are confirmed.
- Manual corrections are authorized and documented.

## Step-by-Step Admin Procedure

1. Filter Visitors and review anyone remaining.
2. Search for expected late-stay members.
3. Confirm unexpected checked-in people with the watch team.
4. Correct missed scans only when authorized.
5. Recheck counts.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Only expected people remain checked in.

Caution: Someone is expected to remain, but the reason is not written down.

Stop: An unexpected visitor or member remains unresolved.

## What To Confirm After Action

Confirm Presence counts match who is physically on site.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if people counts do not match reality.
