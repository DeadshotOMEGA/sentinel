# Actions: Transfer DDS

![Transfer DDS action](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-quick-actions-focus.png)

Transfer DDS moves Duty Day Staff responsibility.

## What This Is

Transfer DDS moves Duty Day Staff responsibility.

## When To Use It

Use it only after confirming both current and incoming DDS expectations.

## What To Check

- Current DDS.
- Incoming DDS identity.
- Whether handover is expected.
- Whether the incoming person accepts responsibility.

## Step-by-Step Admin Procedure

1. Read current DDS status.
2. Confirm incoming DDS identity.
3. Confirm handover expectations.
4. Complete transfer or handover.
5. Recheck DDS status.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: DDS block shows the correct responsible person.

Caution: Incoming DDS is expected but not yet checked in.

Stop: Transfer is unclear or not accepted.

## What To Confirm After Action

Confirm DDS status and Presence after transfer.

## When To Escalate

Escalate disputed DDS handover.
