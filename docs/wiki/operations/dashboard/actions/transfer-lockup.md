# Actions: Transfer Lockup

![Transfer Lockup action](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/dashboard-lockup-ready-state.png)

Transfer Lockup moves lockup responsibility to another qualified checked-in member.

## What This Is

Transfer Lockup moves lockup responsibility to another qualified checked-in member.

## When To Use It

Use it when responsibility is moving before lockup or checkout.

## What To Check

- Current holder.
- Recipient identity.
- Recipient qualification and checked-in state.
- Recipient acceptance.

## Step-by-Step Admin Procedure

1. Confirm current holder.
2. Select a qualified checked-in recipient.
3. Confirm the recipient accepts responsibility.
4. Complete the transfer.
5. Recheck Lockup Holder.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Lockup Holder updates to the correct recipient.

Caution: Recipient is qualified but acceptance has not been confirmed.

Stop: Recipient is not present, not qualified, or not accepting responsibility.

## What To Confirm After Action

Confirm holder block and person card after transfer.

## When To Escalate

Escalate disputed lockup transfer.
