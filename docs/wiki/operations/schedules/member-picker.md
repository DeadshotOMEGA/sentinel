# Schedule Member Picker

> Audience: Schedulers and admins

![Schedule member picker modal](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/schedules-member-picker.png)

## What This Is

Member Picker selects eligible members for DDS or Duty Watch assignment.

## When To Use It

- Assigning DDS.
- Assigning a Duty Watch position.
- Adding or replacing a member in a night override.

## What To Check

- Search result identity and service number.
- Qualification filters match the role.
- The member is not already assigned where conflict rules apply.

## Step-By-Step Procedure

1. Open Assign or Change from the schedule card.
2. Search by name or service number.
3. Check rank, name, service number, and qualifications.
4. Select the member only after confirming identity.
5. Review the card after the picker closes.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: The selected member is eligible, available, and assigned to the correct role.

Caution: Similar names can lead to wrong assignments.

Stop: Do not select a member whose eligibility or availability is uncertain.

## Confirm After Action

- The schedule card displays the selected member.
- Published/draft status is correct after assignment.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if the correct member does not appear in the picker or qualification filter.
