# DDS And Duty Watch Scheduling

> Audience: Schedulers, DDS, admins

![Schedules page overview](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/schedules-overview.png)

## What This Is

Schedules plan DDS and Duty Watch coverage before those assignments become live operational expectations.

## When To Use It

- Plan weekly DDS and Duty Watch assignments.
- Review coverage gaps by week, month, or quarter.
- Publish assignments after review.

## What To Check

- Sentinel says the selected date range and draft/published state.
- The real staffing plan confirms the same people and coverage.
- Required roles are filled or intentionally marked unresolved.

## Step-By-Step Procedure

1. Open Schedules.
2. Choose week, month, or quarter view.
3. Navigate to the correct period.
4. Review DDS and Duty Watch cards.
5. Assign eligible members.
6. Publish only after coverage has been checked.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Coverage is visible, correct, and published when ready.

Caution: Draft coverage is planning information, not final operational direction.

Stop: Do not publish when names, dates, or required positions are unresolved.

## Confirm After Action

- Published badges appear where expected.
- Dashboard readiness reflects the planned Duty Watch when the date arrives.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if required coverage cannot be filled.
