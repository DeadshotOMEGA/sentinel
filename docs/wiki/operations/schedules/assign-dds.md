# Assign DDS

> Audience: Schedulers and admins

![DDS schedule card](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/schedules-assign-dds.png)

## What This Is

Assign DDS sets the weekly Duty Day Staff responsibility that Dashboard operators use for daily readiness and lockup handoff.

## When To Use It

- Plan weekly DDS coverage.
- Replace an incorrect DDS assignment before publishing.
- Confirm who should own daily DDS duties for the week.

## What To Check

- Sentinel says the selected week and current DDS assignment.
- The real duty roster confirms the assigned member.
- The member is eligible and available.

## Step-By-Step Procedure

1. Open the correct week.
2. Find the DDS card.
3. Choose Assign or Change.
4. Pick the correct eligible member.
5. Publish after review when the assignment is final.
6. Tell affected DDS if the assignment changes.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: One qualified DDS is assigned for the correct week.

Caution: DDS assignment influences daily operations and lockup responsibility expectations.

Stop: Do not assign a DDS who is unavailable or not authorized for the role.

## Confirm After Action

- The DDS card shows the expected member.
- The status badge shows Draft or Published as intended.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if no eligible DDS is available.
