# Assign Duty Watch

> Audience: Schedulers and admins

![Duty Watch schedule card](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/schedules-assign-duty-watch.png)

## What This Is

Assign Duty Watch builds the base roster for required watch positions and shows whether coverage is complete.

## When To Use It

- Fill Duty Watch positions for a week.
- Replace or remove a member before publishing.
- Review uncovered positions.

## What To Check

- Sentinel says required positions and current assignments.
- The real roster confirms availability and qualifications.
- Released or unfilled positions are intentional and visible.

## Step-By-Step Procedure

1. Open the correct week.
2. Review required positions in the Duty Watch card.
3. Assign eligible members to each position.
4. Mark unavoidable gaps clearly according to local procedure.
5. Review draft coverage before publishing.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Every required position is filled or visibly unresolved.

Caution: Published Duty Watch is what operators rely on for readiness.

Stop: Do not fill a position with an ineligible member to remove a warning.

## Confirm After Action

- Required count is satisfied or escalated.
- Names and positions are correct.

## When To Escalate

Escalate uncovered required positions before publishing or daily operations.
