# Schedules Week View

> Audience: Schedulers and admins

![Schedules two-week planning view](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/schedules-week-view.png)

## What This Is

Week View is the main editing view. It shows the selected week and the following week so handoff gaps are easier to spot.

## When To Use It

- Assign DDS for a week.
- Build the base Duty Watch roster.
- Compare adjacent weeks before publishing.

## What To Check

- Selected week is correct.
- The second column is the following week.
- Draft or published state is understood before editing.

## Step-By-Step Procedure

1. Open Week view.
2. Use the week picker to choose the correct Monday.
3. Review both week columns.
4. Assign DDS and Duty Watch in the correct column.
5. Publish only the schedule you have checked.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Both weeks show expected coverage and status.

Caution: It is easy to edit the wrong week if the picker is not checked.

Stop: Do not publish a week you have not reviewed column by column.

## Confirm After Action

- The week label and card statuses match the intended schedule.
- Assignments appear in the correct week column.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if the week picker or displayed assignments look inconsistent.
