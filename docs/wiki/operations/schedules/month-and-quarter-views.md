# Month And Quarter Schedule Views

> Audience: Schedulers and admins

![Schedules month and quarter planning view](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/schedules-month-quarter-view.png)

## What This Is

Month and quarter views help scan coverage patterns and gaps over longer periods. They are for planning visibility, while Week view is the main editing surface.

## When To Use It

- Look for recurring gaps.
- Prepare ahead for leave, events, or high-demand periods.
- Brief leadership on upcoming coverage.

## What To Check

- Selected month or quarter is correct.
- Weeks with missing coverage stand out.
- You return to Week view before editing assignments.

## Step-By-Step Procedure

1. Choose Month or Quarter.
2. Navigate to the planning period.
3. Scan for blank or draft coverage.
4. Open or switch to the week that needs editing.
5. Use Week view for detailed assignment work.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: The long-range pattern makes gaps visible early.

Caution: Broad views can hide position-level detail.

Stop: Do not assume a week is ready based only on a broad view.

## Confirm After Action

- Any gap found is reviewed in Week view.
- Known future constraints are recorded or escalated.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if repeated gaps appear across the month or quarter.
