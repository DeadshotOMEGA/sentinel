# Publish And Edit Schedules

> Audience: Schedulers and admins

![Schedule publish and edit controls](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/schedules-publish-edit.png)

## What This Is

Publishing makes a draft schedule operationally visible. Returning to draft allows controlled correction of a published assignment.

## When To Use It

- Coverage has been checked and is ready.
- A published schedule needs a correction.
- DDS or Duty Watch coverage should become official for operations.

## What To Check

- All names, dates, and positions are correct.
- Required positions are filled or escalated.
- Night overrides are reviewed before publishing.

## Step-By-Step Procedure

1. Review the selected week.
2. Confirm DDS and Duty Watch cards.
3. Check draft warnings and required-position badges.
4. Click Publish when ready.
5. If changes are needed later, return to draft, correct, and republish.

## Good / Caution / Stop Conditions

Good: Published schedule matches the real duty plan.

Caution: Publishing too early can mislead Dashboard operators.

Stop: Do not publish unresolved required gaps without escalation.

## Confirm After Action

- Status badge shows Published.
- Dashboard and related views reflect the expected coverage when applicable.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if a published schedule is wrong and the correction affects current operations.
