# Kiosk Operations

> Audience: Duty Day Staff (DDS), supervisors, kiosk operators

![Kiosk operational page with member scan and visitor services](/uploads/wiki-dashboard/operations/kiosk-operations-main.png)

## What This Is

Kiosk Operations is the front entrance attendance surface. Members use the external badge reader, visitors use Visitor Services, and DDS monitors whether kiosk activity matches the real people at the entrance.

The kiosk is not a replacement for DDS judgment. It records what happened, then DDS confirms that the record matches reality.

## When To Use It

- Members are arriving or leaving through the front entrance.
- Visitors need to sign in or out.
- DDS needs to supervise high-traffic attendance.
- The scanner or kiosk needs a readiness check.
- Dashboard Presence needs confirmation after kiosk activity.

## What To Check

- The header shows the correct kiosk location.
- The connection pill is Connected.
- Today's lead/DDS is correct.
- Member Scan says the scanner is ready, paused for visitor sign-in, or clearly unavailable.
- Visitor Services shows active visitor counts and sign-out options.

## Step-By-Step DDS Procedure

1. Confirm the kiosk location is the expected station.
2. Confirm the kiosk is connected before relying on scan results.
3. Keep the badge reader clear and visible.
4. Let members scan their own badges whenever possible.
5. Start Visitor Sign-In only for a real visitor with an approved reason.
6. Use visitor sign-out when a visitor leaves.
7. If the kiosk pauses member scanning for a visitor flow, finish or cancel that visitor flow before resuming member scans.
8. Confirm important changes in Dashboard Presence.

## Good / Caution / Stop

Good: Kiosk is connected, scans are accepted, visitors are checked out when they leave, and Dashboard Presence agrees with the entrance activity.

Caution: Kiosk is connected but Dashboard looks stale. Check System Status and refresh before repeating scans or making manual corrections.

Stop: Do not continue kiosk operations if the station is disconnected, the wrong kiosk location is shown, visitor identity is unclear, or the scanner records the wrong person.

## Confirm After Action

- Member arrivals and departures appear in Dashboard Presence.
- Visitor sign-ins and sign-outs change the visitor count.
- DDS, Duty Watch, and lockup readiness still match who is actually on site.
- Scanner failures are reported instead of worked around silently.

## When To Escalate

Escalate if the kiosk loses connection, the badge reader repeatedly fails, visitor sign-out cannot be completed, a visitor remains active at lockup, or Sentinel's record conflicts with the person in front of DDS.
